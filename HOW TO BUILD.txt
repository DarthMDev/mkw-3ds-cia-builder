MARIO KART WII FOR 3DS: BUILD YOUR CIA
======================================

This folder holds no game files. It builds the CIA from your own disc, on
your own computer.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- Your Mario Kart Wii disc, USA version (RMCE01), dumped as .iso or .wbfs.
  RVZ or NKit dumps: convert them to ISO in Dolphin first.
- A Mac with about 15 GB free and an internet connection.
- These free tools, installed once:
    Xcode command line tools   in Terminal: xcode-select --install
    Python 3.11 or newer       https://www.python.org/downloads/
    Node.js (LTS)              https://nodejs.org
    devkitPro, 3DS tools       https://devkitpro.org/wiki/Getting_Started
                               then in Terminal: sudo dkp-pacman -S 3ds-dev


BUILD
-----
1. Double-click "Build MKW CIA.command".
   If macOS blocks it: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
2. Drag your disc image into the window and press Return.
3. Wait about an hour. Your CIA is saved here:
       build/release/mkw-3ds.cia

If it stops partway, double-click it again; finished steps are skipped.
If it fails, share build-cia-log.txt from this folder when asking for help.


INSTALL
-------
Needs a 3DS with custom firmware (https://3ds.hacks.guide).
1. Copy mkw-3ds.cia to your SD card.
2. Install it with FBI. Mario Kart Wii appears on the HOME Menu.
3. For sound, run DSP1 once to dump your sound firmware.

Old 3DS and 2DS run at 30 FPS. New 3DS and New 2DS run at 60 FPS
(30 FPS with the 3D slider up).
